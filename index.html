FROM PIXELS TO THREAD

CrossStitchify

Turn a favorite image into your next cross stitch project.

1. Design your pattern

Each square is one full cross stitch. Images fit without stretching; unused space becomes white stitches. Larger grids preserve more detail.

2. Stitch your image

Rows run top to bottom, columns left to right. Bold lines mark every 10 stitches.

Thread color key

Match these approximate colors to your own embroidery floss; these are not manufacturer thread codes.

    Before you begin

    1. Choose Aida fabric and embroidery floss matching the color key. Leave extra fabric around all sides for framing.
    2. Find the center of the chart and your fabric to position the design. The row instructions count from the chart’s top-left corner.
    3. Use two strands of floss on 14-count Aida. Make one X per square, keeping the top diagonal in the same direction.
    4. Follow the chart symbols or row instructions below, changing thread at each color change. Secure thread ends on the back.
